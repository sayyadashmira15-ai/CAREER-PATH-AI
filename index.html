How It Works

Four steps to a clear career direction

No guesswork. Each step builds on the previous one until you have a plan you can act on.

  1. 1

    Build Your Profile

    Add your education, interests, and current skills so the system knows where you are starting from.

  2. 2

    Take Assessment

    Complete a short, structured assessment that measures your strengths and career preferences.

  3. 3

    Get AI Analysis

    Receive personalized career recommendations and a clear breakdown of the skills you still need.

  4. 4

    Follow Your Roadmap

    Work through a step-by-step plan and track your progress toward your target career.
